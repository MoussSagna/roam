## Contradictions detected (still open)

1. **Onboarding answers.** `MVP_SCOPE.md` §2 and the `UserPreference` type (interests, preferred activities, usual
   budget, maximum distance, usual company) vs. the built onboarding (mood, time, budget, location, interests, in memory
   only). Documented in `domain/DOMAIN_OVERVIEW.md`; which one is the target is undecided.
2. **Vocabularies.** `Mood` (calm, discover, energetic…) vs. onboarding mood tiles (curious, relaxed, sporty… — mood and
   company mixed) vs. `JourneyMood`; budgets `BudgetRange` (< 10, 10–25, 25–50, 50+) vs. onboarding (< 10, 10–30, 30–50,
   > 50) vs. journey (free/low/medium/high); durations (30 min…3 h+) vs. onboarding (< 1 h … > 4 h) vs. journey (1 h …
   day); plus the Data Foundation enrichment enums. None is wrong in its screen; no shared vocabulary is decided.
3. **Feedback.** Per-experience Love it / Like it / Meh / Not for me + reasons (MVP scope, types only) vs. per-journey
   1–5 stars + comment (built, D-83). See `domain/FEEDBACK.md`.
4. **Itinerary vs. Journey.** The product documents and the planned data domains say "itinerary" (`Itinerary`,
   `ItineraryStep`, `/itineraries`); the app built "journey" and `types/itinerary.ts` is unused. See `domain/JOURNEY.md`.
5. **MVP mobile navigation.** `MVP_SCOPE.md` lists Home · Discover · Favorites · Profile; the product owner replaced
   Favorites with Parcours (D-81, noted in the scope as an implementation note). The scope itself was not rewritten.
6. **Scoring formulations.** Conceptual weights (`07`) vs. the `score = …` sum (`06`); compatible, kept side by side in
   `RECOMMENDATION.md` with a note.
